# Brand And Legal Risk Notes

This is not legal advice. It is an operating checklist for page planning.

## Higher-Risk Terms

- Wordle
- Scrabble
- Words With Friends
- NYT Spelling Bee

## Rules

- Do not use a brand/game name as the site brand.
- Use brand/game names only descriptively where needed to match search intent.
- Add a neutral disclaimer on game-specific pages.
- Avoid logos, trade dress, official-looking UI, or claims of affiliation.
- Consider generic alternatives where possible: five-letter word finder, tile word finder, spelling bee-style solver.

## Safer Core Brand Field

The site should brand around generic language:

- word unscrambler
- word finder
- letter solver
- word puzzle solver

## Review Before Launch

Game-specific pages should be reviewed before publication, especially title tags, H1s, disclaimers, and metadata.
