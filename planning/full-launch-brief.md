# Full Launch Brief - Word Unscrambler New Site

## Decision

Build a full first-launch website around the core keyword `word unscrambler` and the authority field `letter-based word solving tools`.

This is not a thin MVP. The first public launch should include 50 planned pages, each with its own SERP/PAA framework and content brief.

## Positioning

For people stuck in word games, puzzles, or letter constraints, the site helps them turn known letters, patterns, clues, and game rules into usable word answers quickly.

## Core Keyword

- Primary head term: `word unscrambler`
- Supporting head terms: `word finder`, `anagram solver`, `scrabble word finder`, `wordle solver`, `crossword solver`
- Selected first-launch keyword volume: 11,058,100 SEMrush US searches/month

## User Scenario

The user is already doing something: playing Wordle, Scrabble, Words With Friends, a crossword, a Jumble, a classroom word scramble, or another word puzzle. They are stuck and want a fast, low-friction answer or hint. They do not want a long article before the tool.

## Launch Page Set

- Total first-launch pages: 50
- Core/high-priority pages: 15
- Support/variant pages: 35
- SERP/PAA frameworks: one per page in `serp-paa/`
- Content briefs: one per page in `content-briefs/`

## Differentiation

- Tool above the fold on every tool page.
- Mobile-first input, because users often search while playing.
- Clear distinction between using all letters, containing letters, pattern matching, and game-specific rules.
- Fast copyable results, grouped by length and score where useful.
- User-first explanations: what to enter, how to filter, why a word appears.
- SERP/PAA complete coverage without filler copy.

## Launch Readiness Gate

Do not launch until these are true:

- Every page has a SERP/PAA framework file.
- Every page has a content brief.
- Core solver engine supports the shared controls in `engine-spec.md`.
- Brand/game-specific pages include neutral descriptive language and disclaimers.
- Sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs, analytics, GSC, and schema are ready.
- At least the top 15 pages have final edited copy, not raw AI draft copy.

## Post-Launch Measurement

- 0-14 days: indexing, crawlability, sitemap submission.
- 14-30 days: GSC impressions by page/query.
- 30-60 days: CTR, ranking lift, cluster signals.
- 60-90 days: continue, expand, consolidate, or revise based on hit rate.
