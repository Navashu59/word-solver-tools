Scrabble Cheat

Find high-value Scrabble and word-game plays from your rack letters, blanks, and board constraints. Use the controls below and get usable answers without leaving the page.

Use this page when you has a rack of tiles and needs a valid scoring word.

Tool Intro

Enter your tiles, including blanks, to find valid words for your rack and sort them by score or length. The goal is to get usable answers quickly, without reading a long explanation first.

How To Use It

Enter the letters, clue, pattern, or grid you already have.

Add any constraints, such as word length, known positions, required letters, or excluded letters.

Review the results and use filters to remove words that cannot fit your game.

Copy the answer or use the result as a hint before returning to the puzzle.

Example

Example: enter `T A R E ? S` and use `?` for a blank tile. Then sort by score to find stronger plays before checking the board.

What The Results Mean

Results should be grouped so you can make a decision fast. Shorter words, longer words, score-based words, and pattern matches should not be mixed together without filters. If no result appears, remove one constraint or check whether a letter was entered in the wrong position.
