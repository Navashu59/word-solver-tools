You’re staring at a honeycomb of seven letters, one in the center, and you need every valid word that uses that center letter. This tool is for that moment: you want to find words fast, not read a dictionary. Enter your letters, set any length or pattern filters, and get a clean list you can scan or copy.

Why the center letter is non-negotiable

In Spelling Bee, every word must include the center letter. That’s the rule. If you’re stuck, start by listing short words that contain that letter - two- and three-letter words often unlock longer ones. The solver does this instantly, so you can see what you missed.

How to use filters without overcomplicating it

You don’t have to fill every field. Just type the center letter and the six outer letters. Then, if you want, narrow results by:

  • Minimum or maximum word length
  • Words that start with a certain letter
  • Words that contain (or exclude) a specific letter
  • Words that end with a certain letter

This is useful when you already have a few words and want to avoid duplicates.

Pangrams: the 7-point bonus words

A pangram uses all seven letters at least once. It’s worth 7 extra points in the game. The solver flags pangrams automatically, so you can spot them first. If you’re close to Genius rank, pangrams are your best shortcut.

Using the list as a hint or as answer candidates

Not ready to see everything? Use the result list carefully: look at lengths, starting letters, or one small section at a time instead of reading every candidate. That keeps the page useful as a nudge rather than a full spoiler.

What to do when no results appear

If the list is empty, double-check that you entered the center letter correctly. Also make sure you didn’t accidentally exclude a letter that’s actually in the puzzle. Try removing one filter at a time until words show up.

Spelling Bee example

Put the required center letter first, then enter the other six letters. Filter out short words if your puzzle has a minimum length, and scan longer words for possible pangrams.

Before you count the word

Before you count a word, confirm it uses the center letter and meets the puzzle minimum length. For pangram hunting, scan longer results first and check whether all seven letters appear.
