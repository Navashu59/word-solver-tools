Privacy

Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for Word Solver Tools, including how puzzle input and basic site usage are handled.

Word Solver Tools is a static website designed to run word-solving features in your browser. The current tools do not require registration or account login.

Puzzle input

Letters, patterns, and filters you type into the tools are processed in your browser for the current session. They are not needed for account creation or personal identification.

Analytics and hosting

Google Analytics and hosting providers may collect standard technical information such as page views, device type, browser type, referring pages, approximate location, and request logs. Puzzle inputs are not required for account creation or personal identification.

Contact

Use the contact page for privacy-related questions after the production domain is connected.

Word Solver Tools is an independent collection of word solving tools. Game names are used descriptively and do not imply affiliation.
